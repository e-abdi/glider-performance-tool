Glider Performance

Every glider in the rodeo flew the same plan: a shakedown, then fast, slow, intermediate and drift blocks, then a short repeat of slow, intermediate and fast at the end (the mini-modes, right of the hairline in each panel). Because the modes are shared, the differences below are differences between the platforms, not between missions. Numbers come from output/glider_performance_summary.xlsx; each panel keeps its own y-axis, because a watt and a cubic centimetre do not belong on one scale.

Efficiency

Two sections above ask what a kilometre cost and how much each system recorded. This divides one by the other: how far a glider went, and how much it wrote, for every kilowatt‑hour it spent. Up and to the right is more of both for the same energy. Neither axis is the answer — a platform can buy data with distance, and the fastest way to look efficient on the vertical axis is to record at a high bit rate.
